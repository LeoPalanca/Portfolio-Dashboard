How to read this screen

Cash and costs. The cash budget is the lower of total cash and available funds, clamped at zero. Whole-share estimates reserve $1 per purchase. Actual commissions, spread, FX and sale costs may differ. Settlement and available USD still need checking. No percentage position cap is applied; fractional eligibility is unverified.

Data freshness. Quotes are public Yahoo Finance reference prices, possibly from the previous session. Missing prices, missing FX and prices older than four days have unknown affordability. Account snapshots older than 30 minutes are marked stale. Reference prices cannot be used as executable quotes.

Universe and agent. This dated market-cap watchlist includes separate share classes, is not the S&P 100 index and is not refreshed automatically. The agent sees public market data and a locally selected candidate list, without your cash balance or account identity. Manual purchases require a fresh broker preview and your confirmation. Research and chat cannot submit orders. Autonomous entries remain disabled.
